# OSD notes

Measurements behind Crisp's own on-screen display (OSDBannerService, OSDBannerPanel, OSDGlassTuning, OSDBannerView), fitted frame by frame against the system's brightness and volume capsule on 26.5.1 unless a macOS 27 delta is called out; change a tuned value by remeasuring against the native HUD, not by taste.

## Capsule geometry

Measured from the native capsule on 26.5.1, at rest: 10 pt from the right screen edge, 10 pt below the menu bar, corner radius 20 with a continuous curve, view size 292 x 64. Confirmed against inset 11 by reading the lit columns of both capsules over a banded backdrop: the HUD's run ends one pixel further right, and both runs are 292 wide. Measure at rest, not mid-settle: the capsule drops from above over the first half second, and a frame caught mid-settle reads 2 pt narrower, 1 pt shorter and rounder than it ends up. macOS 27's HUD sits 17 pt from the edge instead of 10, measured on the same screen with both capsules up; older releases keep 10. The banner centres on Crisp's menu bar item, under the menu bar, and never sits closer than the trailing inset to either side edge; measured on the system HUD, the brightness capsule's centre sits on the Display control's to half a point, and the volume capsule sits at the trailing inset because centring it on the Sound control would take it past the screen edge.

## Capsule tone

The system HUD's tone reads as one grey over the backdrop, following the line 0.657 x backdrop + 31 on a flat backdrop. Alpha 0.343 with white 0.355 under it draws that same slope and offset. Measured over a black, a mid grey and a light backdrop the banner lands on 31, 129 and 184, the HUD's own three. macOS 27's capsule is slightly lighter: measured settled over backdrops of 0 to 255 its body follows 0.675 x backdrop + 32, against 26.5.1's 0.657 x backdrop + 31.

## Backdrop saturation

A grey scrim at alpha 0.343 keeps 0.657 of the backdrop's own colour; the HUD keeps 1.26 of it, so an unsaturated scrim left a coloured window behind the banner noticeably duller than behind the HUD. Measured on four saturated backdrops the HUD lands on the same brightness line as this scrim to a tenth of a level, and multiplies what colour is left by 1.26 every time, so the sample needs saturating by 1.26 / 0.657 after the grey. Over a strong green the HUD reads 17, 168, 55 and so does this.

## Reduce transparency

Accessibility > Display > Reduce Transparency, which the system's own capsule follows and this one has to follow with it. Measured on 26.5.1 over flat backdrops of 0, 115, 179 and 255 the HUD draws 26, 54, 70 and 89 with the setting on, a line of 0.247 in + 26 where it otherwise draws 0.657 in + 31: much darker. It still tracks what is behind it (a heavier blur and a darker line, not a flat fill): the backdrop keeps none of its detail (0.09, 0.17, 0.19 and 0.26 of the energy a page of text carries at 2, 4, 8 and 16 pixels, against 1.00, 2.26, 3.15 and 4.23 with the setting off). The colour survives at a lower saturation: measured the same way over a green, a red and a blue, the HUD keeps 1.65 of what the line leaves, not 1.92. The blur radius of 20 is enough to leave the backdrop no detail at all, matching the HUD. The close badge under the same setting is a flat disc with no rim, the same over every backdrop, and still follows the appearance: measured over a black and a white backdrop in both, disc 242 with a 122 cross in the light appearance, 20 with a 149 cross in the dark.

## Backdrop blur radius

The HUD softens its backdrop, which is most of what tells the two apart over a real window. Nothing public blurs that way, so the layer samples its backdrop at reduced resolution, like the HUD, and blurs the smaller sample. The measure used is the energy in each scale band inside the capsule, over the same page of text behind both: band k is boxmean(k) minus boxmean(2k). This is cumulative (the energy above each scale piles every finer band into each number), so raw cumulative numbers come out in the same ratio and say nothing; a sparse page also lets bare strips fall between lines, moving the HUD's own number 16 percent run to run, so the page has to be dense. Across 1-2, 2-4, 4-8, 8-16 and 16-32 pixels the HUD reads 2.56, 2.65, 3.21, 3.37 and 3.25: nearly flat, and no single gaussian radius holds both ends. 2.0 is three and a half times too soft at 1-2 pixels; 1.0 lands the two fine bands (2.33 and 2.71) and runs about half again too sharp at the coarse ones, and is the best of the sweep by a wide margin. What would fit is a mix, about 43 percent of the unblurred half-resolution sample over 57 percent of a heavy blur, reproducing all five bands, but it cannot be built: three mechanisms were tried and are all inert. The glass filter's own five-level blur pyramid (inputBlurRadius with inputBlurOpacity0...4 and inputBlurDistance0...4) changes nothing at any radius, with or without distances or the face; the layer's opacity does not mix in a sharp share either, since at 0.90 the profile is identical to 1.0; and stacking two backdrop layers does not mix, because the upper one samples what is already composited below it. The radius is also not a smooth dial: it is quantised somewhere inside the filter, 0.6 measures as unblurred and 0.8 is far sharper than 1.0. macOS 27 softens the backdrop far more than 26 did: measured as the share of a bar pattern that survives behind the capsule, 8 pt from crest to crest, the system draws 0.29 there and 1.0 drew 0.57. That dial is still not smooth either, 1.1 through 1.8 all land between 0.37 and 0.41, and 2.0 is the first value that meets it at 0.27.

## Rim colour and glow

The rim colour is added on rather than blended over, with the same line along the top and bottom edge. Over a flat backdrop the system's two edges read alike: over 29 its top row is 136 and its bottom 134 with the body at 57, and over 199 both clip at 255 with the body at 172. A white blended over cannot draw that pair, since the 0.40 that lands on 136 over the dark backdrop draws 205 over the light one; an added line holds the same 79 levels on both. Over a busy backdrop the system's top row does run brighter than its bottom (125 levels over the body against 80), but that is the glass pulling the backdrop behind it into the edge, not the edge itself. Nothing is drawn down the two rounded ends: the system's ends do run dark, about 65 levels under the body, but that is the glass view's own edge, which this one draws too (57 and 118 against the system's 52 and 116); a dark layer on top reaches a second pixel inwards (63 against the system's 116 over a mid grey), a border the system's capsule does not have. The inner glow sits just inside the top and bottom edge: about 25 levels at the first row under the rim, gone by the sixth. The edge gradient reaches its own colour 40 pt in from a corner (0.14 of 288), which is the rim edge share.

## Refraction amount and height

How far and over how many points the edge bends the backdrop, both fitted against the HUD's own bend, measured as displacement rather than by eye: a stripe backdrop of one period behind both capsules, with the phase of that pattern read column by column in from the edge. The HUD pulls its backdrop 4.8 pt sideways at the strongest point, 8 pt in from the edge, and lets go 26 pt in; this pair draws 4.3 pt at the same place and lets go in the same column, with the HUD's second, weaker shoulder at 15 pt in as well. The height is not a dial: 16 bends nothing at all and 30 folds the backdrop over itself, so it stays at 20 (what the system's own glass carries), and the amount does the fitting; the amounts the system's own glass variants carry, -26 to -80, move this backdrop by well under a point. Refitted on the tone the edge actually shows, which the displacement alone missed: over a backdrop of 16 pt bands, how far each column in from the edge sits off the flat tone the middle of the capsule holds. The HUD reads 33, 27, 22, 20, 19, 18, 13, 11 and 11 levels off at 2 to 10 pt in; -110 read 35, 30, 27, 22, 20, 19, 17, 14 and 12, a quarter strong the whole way in, which is the dark band the eye finds along the bottom edge over a bright window. This amount reads 32, 27, 21, 21, 20, 17, 14, 12 and 11: within a level of the HUD at every column. The refraction filter carries a blur of its own, heavy by default: left alone it takes the banner to 5.2 of the backdrop's fine structure where the HUD leaves 15.4, so it is turned off and the gaussian blur above it does the fitting instead.

## Entry, hold and exit timings

Entry, hold and exit are fitted to the system HUD, measured frame by frame on 26.5.1 as the tone the screen actually shows. The capsule fades in over 0.55 s on a curve with a slow start, while it grows in from 11 pt narrower and 2 pt shorter on each side and settles down 5.5 pt over 0.35 s, easing out. It holds 1 s after the last press, then lifts back and shrinks a little further than it grew from (14 by 3 pt a side) over 0.45 s, and fades on its own curve, which falls fast and tails off: the system's takes 163 ms from four fifths of its tone to one fifth and 291 ms to a twentieth, and this one 163 and 297. One fade carries the whole capsule, grey included: a separate, slower animation on the grey brings the backdrop in ahead of the tone, which reads as the banner arriving sharp. macOS 27 takes its time over the same three stages: measured frame by frame at 60 fps over the same backdrop, with one press and nothing else moving on screen, the system capsule holds its tone 530 ms after it first shows where this one held at 310, and it is off screen 1233 ms after it arrives where this one had gone at 1016. How much smaller the capsule opens: macOS 27 opens from further in, the system capsule is invisible for its first 137 ms and comes into view at 266 pt wide, 28 under its settled 294, and takes until 520 ms to close that. Every curve here was fitted against the native capsule on the same backdrop.

## Menu bar item light

The menu bar item goes dark as the banner starts to leave, not once it has gone, matching the system: measured on the system's own, its item goes dark between 0.85 and 0.95 seconds after the press, while its capsule is still half there. A banner the pointer is on holds itself up, and the system's own item stays lit through that, measured 2.25 seconds after the press.

## Full-screen detection

Measured on 26.5.1 over a full-screen window, the HUD centres on the screen's midline to the point instead of hanging under a menu bar item, because the bar and every item on it move to another screen while a space is full screen; without this the banner would fall back to the corner and sit half a screen from the HUD. The signal is a window covering the display exactly, owned by an app with a Dock tile, since only those take a space full screen: a zoomed window stops short of the menu bar and does not match, and nothing covers a display exactly in the ordinary case. An accessory app's window can, though: the Cua Driver desktop tool keeps an empty overlay over a whole display while it runs, and without the owner check the banner centred on the midline, 708 pt from its item. The answer is cached briefly per display: the window list is a round trip to the window server, usually about 1.3 ms here but measured at 53 ms on a bad run with only thirty windows open, which on a held key is a visible hitch in the fade the press itself is driving. A space takes far longer than the hold to come and go, so a stale cached answer inside it cannot be wrong in practice.

## Backdrop layer

Nothing public blurs this gently: every NSGlassEffectView material and every NSVisualEffectView material takes the same 204-level step below 20, against the HUD's 37, and a Core Image filter over the backdrop cannot touch it, because the window server composites the backdrop, not this process. windowServerAware is what the system's own glass sets on its backdrop layer (dumped from a live NSGlassEffectView): without it the sample is the app's own view of what is behind the window, which goes stale when nothing on screen changes.

## Badge tone

The close badge does not sit on the capsule: it samples the desktop the way the capsule does and lays its own line over it. Measured over flat backdrops of five tones, its disc reads 187, 203, 230 and 249 where the desktop reads 56, 116, 183 and 255, which is out = 0.31 in + 170. No white at any alpha over the capsule can draw that: it takes alpha 0.65 to land on 203 in the middle and 0.90 to land on 249 at the top, so a flat badge over a light desktop reads as a grey disc where the system's is nearly clear. The filters do not realise what they are asked for either (0.31 and 0.667 measure as 0.219 and 0.648), so both were fitted against the system's badge over the same backdrops. The system's badge follows the system appearance independently of the capsule, which is why there are two pairs here: over the same grey backdrop its disc reads 187 in light and 88 in dark. Swept over four backdrops in each appearance, in one run per pair on the same screen, its line is 0.431 in + 150 light and 0.510 in + 23 dark, and these pairs land on 0.433 in + 150 and 0.510 in + 21, within 6 levels at every backdrop. Still not matched: near the far end of each appearance the system sometimes swaps to the other look, a light disc in dark over a backdrop of 166 and a dark one in light under about 35. It is not a fixed threshold, since the same 166 backdrop left the dark look in place on a later run; following it needs the backdrop's own level, which only Screen Recording gives. The badge's blur is heavier than the capsule's: over a checkerboard the system's badge leaves 3 levels of the backdrop's 59, where the capsule leaves 15.

## Window margin and badge

The window reaches 30 pt past the capsule on every side: the close badge hangs 2.5 points over the capsule's top left corner and its shadow reaches 30 past it, and a window cut to the capsule would clip both off. The badge itself is centred 6.5 points in from the capsule's corner and 18 points across, measured on the system HUD over a flat backdrop.

## Badge shadow

The shadow the badge sits on is what makes it read as an object over a light desktop rather than a disc that disappears into it: a radial gradient, masked to the outside of the disc, since the disc is a backdrop sample and lets whatever is under it through (which took ten levels off its own tone). The alphas are the system's own, read as a drop profile: over a flat backdrop, how far the screen sits below it ring by ring, in the quadrant up and left of the disc where nothing but desktop is behind. Taken that way the system's shadow is a black at 0.079 one point outside the disc, 0.044 five points out, 0.024 eleven out and 0.007 twenty-two out, the same profile over two backdrops. Read that way and not by eye: the near end is what reads as a drawn circle, and a tail a third to a half too strong from eleven points out is what reads too wide; a blur instead of a gradient does not do it either, since the tail is far longer than any blur's. The dark appearance carries its own, weaker profile: 0.048 a point out where light has 0.079, and gone by twenty points out where light still carries 0.024.

## Badge glyph

The glyph's weight is what fits it: the system's covers 29 pixels at 1x and its ink adds up to about 1900 levels below the disc, where 9 point bold covers 29 at 1975 and every lighter weight leaves the X too thin (8.5 regular covers 19 at 1003), half a point over the 9 the ink fit asked for.

## Capsule grow (macOS 27)

The capsule opens narrower and shorter and grows into place, pinned at the middle of its top edge: the system's left edge runs 321 to 308 while its right runs 587 to 600, so the middle stays put and it grows out to both sides and downward. The glass and the rim are resized, not scaled, while the content is drawn at its final size and scaled down into the smaller capsule; both halves are needed. The system's label at 140 ms matches its settled label at 0.970, so the text does ride a scale, but scaling the glass as well draws its refraction band thinner than it settles at, and a thin band is a hard dark line that lifts as the grow ends, reading as the capsule's inner shadow going away; the system holds 10.5 levels under its body throughout.

## Hover hold

The system's own HUD stays up as long as the pointer is there and starts its hold again when it leaves, measured at 0.2, 0.6, 1.0 and 1.45 seconds after the leave.

## Badge fade

The badge fades and only fades, measured on the system HUD over a flat backdrop at 75 frames a second: 0.29 seconds in on an ease-out only a little faster than a straight line, and 0.35 out, running straight. The knob and the fill are not animated at all.

## Rim (macOS 27, OSDBevelView)

Measured at rest, once the capsule has settled: over backdrops of 0, 64, 128, 192 and 255 the body reads 32, 75, 118, 162 and 204, and the top and bottom edge both read 102, 158, 215, 255 and 255. The two rounded ends run dark instead, about 65 levels under the body, drawn by the glass view's own edge rather than here. Catch the capsule before it has settled and none of that is true: at 0.9 s after a single press it is still 6 pt narrow with half the rim, so the measurement holds the key down (ten presses) and shoots half a second after the last one. The lift along the top and bottom is near enough the same 85 levels on every backdrop, so it goes on as an additive layer, the only blend that reaches the capsule at all (the body is composited by the window server, so a CoreImage blend mode here has nothing under it to blend with: measured, soft light drew a third of the lift it should, overlay a sixth). It runs as one horizontal gradient over a ring mask, because the change happens along the width: 40 pt in from a corner the top edge is at its full value and at 20 pt it is half way. Inside the top and bottom edge the system keeps a short glow too: over a 128 backdrop the rows under the rim read 139, 130, 126 and 123 against a body of 118, gone by the sixth.

## Glass entry and exit timing

macOS 27 eases its glass in and out instead of fading a finished one; the grow, the blur, the bend, the tint and the label each come in on their own clock, all measured from the key press rather than the first visible frame. The system draws nothing at all for 120 ms and then runs 0.24, 0.45, 0.55, 0.70, 0.77 and 0.85 of its settled contrast at 160, 200, 240, 280, 320 and 360 ms; on the way out the blur closes in about 180 ms and the tone and label in about 280, while the window takes the rest. The window's own fade (glassWindowInDuration, 0.02 s) is almost nothing: it is the carrier, and the face tint over glassInDuration (0.62 s) is what actually brings the capsule in, since a window still at low alpha lets the sharp desktop through whatever the filter does and the system's first frame already blurs and bends what is behind it. glassInCurve is a gentle toe, then the body of the move, then a long settle, the shape the system's own tone follows over a light backdrop. The content fades over the whole travel (glassContentInDuration 0.72 s), measured over a dark backdrop since a light one hides the ramp under the track's white; glassContentInCurve is slow at both ends, not an ease out, measured over a dark backdrop as the label's brightest pixel against its settled one: the system runs 0.06, 0.12, 0.27, 0.59, 0.85, 0.93 and 0.96 at 0, 60, 120, 200, 300, 400 and 500 ms. The grow (glassGrowInDuration 0.48 s) is fitted to the system capsule's own width: it opens 30 pt narrower and 7 pt shorter than it ends, and is within a point of its full width at 420 ms (27 percent of the way at 53 ms, 47 at 103, 67 at 157, 80 at 209, 93 at 313), while its blur, bend and label keep settling inside it until about 800 ms; glassGrowInCurve is an ease out, full speed at the start decelerating the whole way. The rim (glassRimInDuration 0.55 s) comes up behind the body, not with it: measured on one capture with both capsules over the same backdrop, as the rim's lift over the body divided by the body's own progress, the system's border line is at 0.32 of its share when the body is halfway there, then 0.53, 0.88, 0.94 and 1.00 at 100, 150, 200 and 250 ms. The bright line along the top edge is drawn twice, by OSDBevelView's rim over the glass and by the glass's own key fill highlight (glassHighlight) under it; both ride this same ramp, or half the line would be up from the first frame, where the system's rim carries 0.14 of the body's progress at 100 ms. The settle (glassSettleInDuration 0.7 s) ends where the system's does: frame against frame, the system capsule stops changing between 650 and 700 ms after the press. The exit (glassOutDuration 0.5 s) takes the same steps back, faster at the start with a long tail: the system capsule has 72 percent of its way left at 50 ms, 16 at 250 and the last few at 450, and its window stays opaque until then and is gone at 500. The parts go at their own pace inside that: the system is down to 60 percent of its contrast 130 ms into the exit, and its decay from there is close to a straight line into a long faint tail (glassBlurOutDuration 0.3 s, glassTintOutDuration and glassBendOutDuration and glassRimOutDuration 0.35 s, glassWindowOutDuration 0.5 s). glassExitInset (18 x 4) travels less than the entry: the capsule opens far and closes a little.

## Glass blur, bend and tint

macOS 27's capsule is the system's own glass: one filter that blurs, tints and bends the backdrop together, which is what reads as tinted glass (a blur with a grey laid over it reads as a solid behind glass however well its numbers match on a flat backdrop). glassVariant (2) is the dark variant, the closest of the system's own; its inputs are fitted against the system HUD over a striped and lettered backdrop, the tint to within 2 levels of the HUD's mean in each channel and the bend at both ends, where the HUD wraps the colours outside it into a thin ring. The blur goes in steps and above about 1.25 draws the next one up; the HUD also pulls text outside its top and bottom edges well into the body, which a refraction height of 14 draws and 12 does not. The blur comes in two steps (glassBlurStep 0.3 over glassBlurStepDuration 0.06 s, then to full over glassBlurRiseDuration 0.45 s after a glassBlurRiseDelay of 0.18 s), fitted over the same stripes: the text behind the system capsule keeps its edges for the first 145 ms and loses them over the next 240. A radius under 0.6 draws as none at all. glassInputs' inputBlurOpacity0 (0.8, the view leaves it at 1) lets a little of the sharp backdrop back through the blur, as the system HUD does; the other three blur opacities stay as the view sets them. inputFaceColorMatrixWhite, Black and Saturation (0.79, 0.073, 1.4) are fitted on the plain part of the capsule over the stripes: the system's reads mean 65.1 with a spread of 20.8, and these two land on 64.8 and 21.0; the black point carries the tone, and 0.095 leaves the body four levels bright. glassBlur (0.95 open and closed) is one of two levels the radius can take: 1.1 and 1.25 draw the same thing and 1.3 draws the next level, with the system's capsule between them; at 0.95 the text behind the band keeps a little more structure than the system's (49 to 68 levels against its 54 to 61), while the next level up is three levels brighter and flatter. Open and closed are the same because the lens is full from the first frame; closed is not zero either, since a radius of 0 draws as a wide blur in the window server and anything under 0.6 draws as none. glassBend (open -75, closed 0) comes in flat and bends over the first 180 ms, where the shimmer takes over; the amount rises with the band's depth (glassRefractionHeight) below it, since a deep band at full bend is a soft shadow and a shallow one a hard dark line. The bend ends where the shimmer begins and is done well before the settle, since the system's edge reaches full brightness by 350 ms and cannot until the bend behind it is full; glassBendInCurve is off the mark at once with a long tail. glassRefractionHeight (open 15, closed 0) is the band the bend pulls in over the top and bottom edges: it opens at nothing and grows down from the top, which is the entry's movement, and its settled height also sets how bright the body under the band reads (the system reads 65.5 on the row five to nine under the top edge, where 14 gives 64.1, 15 gives 57.7 and 17 gives 50.6, each higher value darker with a thinner brighter arc). glassRefractionHeightDuration (0.44 s) is short because a shallow band draws a harder line than a deep one and a slow ramp holds that line on screen; the system reaches its own 10 levels under the body by 120 ms and holds it (6.2, 8.0, 12.0 and 10.6 at 0, 60, 120 and 200 ms). glassTint (open 0.88, closed 0) is the face the entry ramps to; closed is nothing since the face brings the entry in, not the window.

## Glass shimmer

Once the grow, the bend and the blur are done, the glass dips its backdrop sampling from 1.0 to glassShimmerScale (0.45) and back, which makes what is behind the glass drift for a moment and then come to rest, over glassShimmerDelay 0.20 s and glassShimmerDuration 0.30 s, returning after glassShimmerReturnDelay 0.50 s over glassShimmerReturnDuration 0.20 s. How deep the dip goes: under about 0.4 the backdrop switches to another sampling level and part of the change arrives in one frame, which over a light window moves the biggest frame's pixels by 25 levels at 0.45 and by 62 at 0.3. The system's settled capsule resolves small text behind it, down to the digits in a window of figures under its top band, which is how the return to sharp is judged.

## Label size and position

Fitted at 2x, where a point is two pixels: the same label drawn at 13, 12.5 and 12 next to the HUD's own, matched to it by sliding one profile over the other, puts the HUD at 12.33, 12.22 and 12.18, and its ascenders (18.5 px against 19.7, 18.9 and 18.1) agree; 13 pt reads slightly wide beside it, so the label uses 12.25 while the glyphs stay at 13. The label colour is explicit white at 85 percent, not .primary, which reads thinner and duller than the HUD's label (peak 243 against its 251 over the same body). The row the smaller label costs is given back in its frame height (16 pt), so the track and glyphs stay on the HUD's rows: the line box at 13 pt is 16 pt tall. The label sits a quarter point down from that box, putting the baseline where the HUD's sits, 22.75 pt under the top of the capsule, measured at 2x on both; macOS 27 carries its label one row higher, measured settled on the same rows.

## Track (inactive vs. key window)

AppKit draws a slider in an inactive window in its inactive state, where the line is a grey darker than its own groove (measured 71 against 82 over a backdrop of 91) and the knob has no glass; only a key window draws the real one, and nothing else reaches it (the tint, trackFillColor, the controlActiveState environment value, an overridden isKeyWindow and a posted key notification all leave the inactive drawing in place). The fill ends on the tick for the level, not at a plain fraction of the track: measured settled on the system HUD, three steps up its fill ends on the third dot and eleven steps up on the eleventh, to a tenth of a pixel at 2x; the top of the range is the exception, running to the end of the track instead of stopping on the last dot. The tick dots' centres sit 9 px in from each end of a 450 px track at 2x (its seventeen dots run 74.5 to 505.5 from the same origin). macOS 27 draws the dots brighter: 28 levels over the body against the 16 that 0.11 draws, measured settled over a mid grey backdrop.
